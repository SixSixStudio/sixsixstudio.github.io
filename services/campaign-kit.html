Campaign Kit

A video on its own is a file. Campaign Kit is everything that makes it earn: the writing, the assets around it, the place it sends people, and the reporting that decides what gets made next. Sold as one package, delivered alongside the video.

Example

Production support

  • Hooks and scripts — twenty angles per concept
  • Voiceover, recorded and directed
  • Burned-in captions, styled to the brand
  • Sound design: effects, drums, atmosphere

Design assets

  • Static ads and carousels
  • Covers and thumbnails for each platform
  • Brand kit: fonts, colours, logo cleanup
  • Reusable templates

Copy & scheduling

  • Captions, hashtags, bios and one-line offers
  • Content calendar
  • Batch production, so one session fills a month

Web presence

  • One-page sites and landing pages
  • Google Business Profile setup
  • Local search copy

Conversion

  • Competitor content audit
  • Performance reporting and what to make next
  • Comment and DM playbook
  • Lead magnet, review cards and follow-up messages

Every project is quoted after a short conversation — what you get, what it costs and when it lands are agreed before anything starts.
