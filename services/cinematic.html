Cinematic

Looking for cinematic visuals? Do you want to turn your content more cinematic or do you have content we could turn into a cinematic masterpiece.

Want to create a cinematic masterpiece or a cinematic ad campaign for social media?

What if you want it to look like a premium ad with cinematic visuals? What if your idea is huge and involves a helicopter landing in a stadium or a car chase in New York City?

Not to worry, we can make short films or long films with cinematic visuals, special effects.

Got an idea for social media for your brand and business NO job is too small or too BIG.

What it covers

  • Interiors and locations you can't book: kitchens, labs, factories, rooftops
  • Drone and aerial movement without a drone
  • Impossible camera paths — through walls, inside bottles, inside machinery
  • Extreme macro: droplets, condensation, powder bursts, pours, fabric weave
  • Product hero shots — floating, rotating, coming apart into components
  • Slow motion: splash, spill, steam, flame, ice
  • Weather and time of day on demand
  • Studio lighting you'd never hire, and set extension that turns a corner into a location

Face & body

  • Face swap — a face onto footage that already exists
  • Full body replacement — build, height or whole figure swapped
  • Talking head — a spokesperson delivering a script they never filmed, lip-synced
  • Virtual try-on — a garment or product on a person who never wore it
  • Character replacement — the actor in a clip replaced entirely
  • Presenter localisation — the same advert, a different face and voice per market

Every project is quoted after a short conversation — what you get, what it costs and when it lands are agreed before anything starts.
