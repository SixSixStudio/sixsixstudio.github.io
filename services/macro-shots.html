Macro Shots

Looking for macro shots? Do you want the detail that makes a product look like it costs more than it does — condensation sliding down a glass, honey leaving the jar, powder coming off a surface, the weave of the fabric they are about to buy?

Do you have footage already that we could pull those shots out of? What if you need a close-up no camera could get: inside the bottle, through the droplet, a splash held in mid-air?

Not to worry, we can shoot one detail or build a whole campaign around it.

Got an idea for social media for your brand and business NO job is too small or too BIG.

What it covers

  • Liquid: pours, drips, splashes, ripples, droplets in free fall
  • Powder and dust: bursts, settling, impact clouds on glass or stone
  • Condensation and steam — cold glass, hot surfaces, breath on metal
  • Texture: fabric weave, leather grain, brushed steel, paper fibre, skin
  • Product detail: stitching, engraving, caps, seals, labels, ingredients
  • Food and drink: cut, pull, melt, crust, foam, ice
  • Insects, flowers and natural surfaces when the story calls for it

Where it earns its keep

  • Beauty and skincare — serum, cream, colour and packaging close up
  • Drinks — spirits, coffee, soft drinks, the pour that sells the taste
  • Food — texture is appetite; the close-up does the work a wide shot can't
  • Jewellery and watches — facets, clasps, engraving, the catch of light
  • Product films and packshots that need one detail shot among the wide ones
  • Openers and stingers — a two-second macro beat to break up a longer edit

Macro is usually the shot that makes the rest of the edit look expensive. It is also the cheapest to add to a project that is already running.
