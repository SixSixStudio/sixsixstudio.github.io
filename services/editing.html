Editing

You have the footage. We make it worth watching: pacing that holds, cuts that land on the beat, occlusion so type and subjects share the frame properly, sound designed rather than dumped, and one grade held across the whole thing.

Short-form editing — 30s

Editing is the service for people who already have material and know it should be better. A shoot that ran long, a phone full of clips, an old advert that no longer fits, a founder talking to camera. The footage does not need to be reshot. It needs to be chosen, ordered and finished — and that is a different craft from shooting it.

What separates a cut that works from one that doesn't is rarely the effect. It is the decision about which frame to leave out, where the cut lands, how the sound carries the eye, and whether one look is held from the first second to the last.

What you send

  • Rushes, phone footage, an old edit, a screen recording
  • Whatever audio exists — or none
  • The platforms it is going to
  • Any brand files you already have

What comes back

  • A finished cut, graded and captioned
  • The same film in every ratio you posted for
  • Cutdowns at the lengths you actually use
  • A master file, not a folder of exports

Who it is for

  • Businesses with footage and no editor
  • Creators who need volume without losing quality
  • Anyone whose last advert underperformed and suspects the edit

What we bring to it

  • Rhythm cutting — every cut lands on the beat
  • Subject occlusion — type passing behind what moves
  • Match cuts — a shape or motion carries the join
  • Speed ramps — slow motion snapped back to real time
  • Time remapping — the same shot, retimed for pace
  • Depth moves — a still frame that travels
  • Seam design — the end of one shot built into the start of the next
  • Sound design — impacts, room tone, transitions
  • One grade — held across every shot
  • Burned-in captions — styled to the brand, timed to the cut
  • Reframes — landscape made vertical without losing the subject
  • Cutdowns — one film into every length you need
  • And more

How it works

  • Send the footage and tell us what it is for
  • A short call, then a written plan: what you get and what it costs
  • Nothing starts until the scope is agreed
  • Fixed quote per project. No price list, no per-minute billing
  • You approve the cut before it is published anywhere

If the footage is not there — nothing usable, wrong light, wrong length — we will say so before quoting rather than charge to rescue it.
