Campaign retainer

The whole job on a rolling month: one campaign idea, the films that carry it, the posting, the site and the search. The number of films is agreed with you month by month — there is no package to fit you into.

Campaign retainer — 33s

Most studios sell a package: this many films for this much money, whether the month needs them or not. A retainer works the other way round. We agree what this month is actually about — an opening, a new service, a quiet season that needs filling — then make the work that carries that one idea everywhere it needs to appear.

The film is the centre of it, not the whole of it. The same idea becomes shorts for the feed, a longer cut for the site, captions for each platform, pages that answer what people search for, and the report at the end that says what it did.

In the month

  • Films cut from one shoot
  • Every platform, sized and captioned
  • Posting on a schedule that holds
  • Site pages built and fixed
  • Search position worked on
  • Brand held to one system
  • Weekly reports, in plain English

How the quantity works

  • Agreed with you at the start of each month
  • Six films is a working example, not a package
  • Heavy months and quiet months both cost what they should
  • Nothing rolls over that was not asked for

Who it is for

  • Businesses that publish nothing because nobody owns it
  • Owners who want the whole job off their desk
  • Anyone who has paid for a film and watched it sit unused

Site & search — worked on every month

Search engine optimisation is not a mystery: it is the work that moves your site up when someone searches for what you sell. A page for each service, opening hours corrected, the location pin fixed, the words people actually type answered on the page. Done monthly, the site stops being a brochure and starts being the first result a customer sees.

  • Pages built — one per service, written for the search
  • Hours and details — corrected everywhere they appear
  • Location pinned — the map result pointed at the door
  • Search position — tracked and climbed, month by month
  • Reports — what moved, in plain English
  • Held across platforms — one brand, everywhere

How it works

  • A short call about the business and what this month needs
  • One written plan: the idea, the films, the posting, the site work, the cost
  • Nothing starts until that plan is agreed
  • You approve each cut before it is published
  • The month is reported on — what went out and what it did

Films, posting, site and search are one conversation on purpose: the film that works is the one the site and the search results are ready to catch.
