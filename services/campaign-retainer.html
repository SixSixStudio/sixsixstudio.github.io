Campaign retainer

Looking for one team to run the month, not a one-off edit?

Do you want the film or films, the posting, the site and the search handled by the same people — one idea carried everywhere it appears, planned at the start of the month and reported at the end of it?

What if you don't know how many films you need until the month starts, and the last thing you want is a twelve-month contract for work that keeps changing shape?

Not to worry. A retainer buys a standing slot in the studio: films built for whatever the month needs — shot by us, cut from footage you already have, or made in-house — sized and captioned for every platform and posted at the hours they perform, site pages built and fixed, search worked on, brand held to one system, and a plain-English report at the end of it. The scope, the deliverables and the fee are agreed in writing before the month starts, retainer clients come before one-off projects, and it runs on 30 days' notice — no year-long lock-in. Media spend and third-party costs sit outside the fee.

Site & search — worked on every month

Search engine optimisation is not a mystery: it is the work that moves your site up when someone searches for what you sell. A page for each service, opening hours corrected, the location pin fixed, the words people actually type answered on the page. Done monthly, the site stops being a brochure and starts being the first result a customer sees.

  • Pages built — one per service, written for the search
  • Hours and details — corrected everywhere they appear
  • Location pinned — the map result pointed at the door
  • Search position — tracked and climbed, month by month
  • Reports — what moved, in plain English
  • Held across platforms — one brand, everywhere

How it works

  • A short call about the business and what this month needs
  • One written plan: the idea, the films, the posting, the site work, the cost
  • Nothing starts until that plan is agreed
  • You approve each cut before it is published
  • The month is reported on — what went out and what it did

Films, posting, site and search are one conversation on purpose: the film that works is the one the site and the search results are ready to catch.
