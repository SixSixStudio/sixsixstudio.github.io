Social Media Clips

Premium vertical video, made from what you already shoot. We take your footage — a product, a service, a day on site — and transform it into the kind of short that stops a scroll: the hook, the pacing, the grade and the finishing detail of the reels you stop to watch yourself. Built for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, sized and finished for each platform.

Social Media Clips is short vertical video for Reels, Shorts and TikTok — built for content creators and for businesses that know they need to post but do not have an editor. Send the footage you already shoot, or have it shot for you, and we turn it into shorts that look made rather than uploaded: hooks built for the first second, cuts landing on the beat, captions designed rather than auto-generated, and the finishing detail that makes a clip stand out in a feed. UGC-style ads, organic posting, product demos, testimonials, offer ads, and one long video cut into a month of shorts.

How it works

  • Send the footage you already have, or have it shot and directed for you
  • A short call, then a written plan: what you get and what it costs
  • Nothing starts until the scope is agreed and signed off
  • Fixed quote per project. No price list, no per-second billing
  • Delivered as a finished set, not a folder of rushes

Every project is quoted after a short conversation — what you get, what it costs and when it lands are agreed before anything starts.
