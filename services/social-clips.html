Social Media Clips

Premium vertical video, made from what you already shoot. We take your footage — a product, a service, a day on site — and transform it into the kind of short that stops a scroll: the hook, the pacing, the grade and the finishing detail of the reels you stop to watch yourself. Built for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, sized and finished for each platform.

Social Media Clips is short vertical video for Reels, Shorts and TikTok — built for content creators and for businesses that know they need to post but do not have an editor. Send the footage you already shoot, or have it shot for you, and we turn it into shorts that look made rather than uploaded: hooks built for the first second, cuts landing on the beat, captions designed rather than auto-generated, and the finishing detail that makes a clip stand out in a feed. UGC-style ads, organic posting, product demos, testimonials, offer ads, and one long video cut into a month of social media clips.

How it works

  1. A
    Get in touch

    One message. Tell us the business and what you want the video to do.

  2. B
    Discuss your idea

    A short call about what you sell, who you are trying to reach, and the idea you have in mind.

  3. C
    Send your content

    Send what you already shoot. If you have nothing shot, we obtain the footage on your behalf.

  4. D
    We create it

    Script, shoot or build, cut, grade, caption — your idea brought to life. You approve the cut.

  5. E
    Add-ons, if you want them

    Automated posting, so each clip goes out at the hours that work. Or a campaign retainer that runs the idea month by month — renewed or cancelled by you.

  6. F
    Delivered

    Your product delivered within the agreed time frame.
