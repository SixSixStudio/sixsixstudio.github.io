Social clips

Short vertical video, made in the formats that actually move people on Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Written to be watched with the sound off, cut to hold attention past the first second, and sized for each platform.

Formats

  • UGC-style ads, shot like a person rather than a brand
  • Hook variant packs — one ad, five to ten openings for testing
  • Organic content packages on a monthly rhythm
  • Repurposing long video, podcasts and webinars into shorts
  • Product demos, unboxings and hands-on footage
  • Testimonial and case-study clips
  • Offer, seasonal and limited-time ads
  • Cutdowns of one master into every length

What you get

  • One master edit, exported for Reels, Shorts, TikTok, feed and landscape
  • Captions burned in, so the clip works with the sound off
  • The hook decided before the edit starts — the first second is built, not left to chance
  • Script, shot direction and edit notes with every cut
  • Titles, captions and hooks saved as templates you can reuse
  • Two rounds of revisions before the final delivery
  • Source files handed over — nothing held back

Motion

  • Rhythm cutting, whip pans and crash zooms
  • Speed ramps — slow motion snapped back to real time
  • Glitch cuts and strobes for impact

Layout & type

  • Split screens and overlay composites
  • Phone mockups, three-up and four-up strips
  • 3D phone rotation, frame inside a frame
  • Captions written and timed for sound-off viewing
  • Kinetic type and words that sit behind the subject

Colour & finish

  • Match cuts, mask reveals and luma wipes
  • Three grades side by side, colour grade split
  • Landscape footage reframed for 9:16
  • Freeze frames and seamless looping

How it works

  • Send the footage you already have, or have it shot and directed for you
  • A short call, then a written plan: what you get and what it costs
  • Nothing starts until the scope is agreed and signed off
  • Music is optional, captions are not — every clip works muted
  • Fixed quote per project. No price list, no per-second billing
  • Delivered as a finished set, not a folder of rushes

Every project is quoted after a short conversation — what you get, what it costs and when it lands are agreed before anything starts.
