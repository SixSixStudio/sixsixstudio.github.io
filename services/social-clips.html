Social clips

Short vertical video, made in the formats that actually move people on Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Written to be watched with the sound off, cut to hold attention past the first second, and sized for each platform.

Social clips is short vertical video for Reels, Shorts and TikTok — built for content creators and for businesses that know they need to post but do not have an editor. Send the footage you already shoot, or have it shot for you, and we turn it into shorts that look made rather than uploaded: hooks built for the first second, cuts landing on the beat, captions that read with the sound off, and the finishing detail that makes a clip stand out in a feed. UGC-style ads, organic posting, product demos, testimonials, offer ads, and one long video cut into a month of shorts.

Editing styles — what we bring to your footage

  • Rhythm cutting — every cut lands on the beat
  • Whip pans — fast transitions between scenes
  • Crash zooms — snap into the detail
  • Speed ramps — slow motion snapped back to real time
  • Glitch cuts — a hard digital interrupt
  • Strobe cuts — flashing impact frames
  • Split screens — two shots, one frame
  • Overlay composites — one clip layered over another
  • Phone mockups — footage playing inside a phone
  • Three-up strips — three clips side by side
  • Four-up grids — four clips in one frame
  • 3D phone rotation — the device turning in space
  • Frame in a frame — a shot inside a shot
  • Burned-in captions — written for sound-off viewing
  • Kinetic type — words that move with the cut
  • Text behind subject — type sitting behind whatever it moves past
  • Match cuts — one shape or motion carries the cut
  • Mask reveals — a shape opening onto the next shot
  • Luma wipes — light pulling one shot into the next
  • Three grades — the same shot, three looks
  • Grade split — two grades across one frame
  • 9:16 reframe — landscape footage made vertical
  • Freeze frames — the action held, dimmed, held
  • Seamless loops — the clip ends where it starts
  • Script and shot planning — direction written before the edit
  • Platform exports — 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 from one master
  • Cutdowns — one video cut into every length you need
  • Template packs — titles, captions and hooks you can reuse
  • Hook-first opening — the reason to keep watching, in second one
  • And more
